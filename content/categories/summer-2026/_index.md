Leaving my Freshman year I was hoping to work over the summer. I loved my past summer internships; they can provide a change of pace, unmatched learning, and an opportunity to revel in a very specific subfield you find interesting. This summer, however, is governed by one thing—a surgery that my orthodontist has been planning since I was 13: Orthognathic surgery (a bilateral sagittal split osteotomy + Le Fort I osteotomy, to be specific). Since February I've been flying home from school roughly every 6 weeks for one appointment or another. My surgery is scheduled for July 24th, about 40% through the summer. With appointments tying me to Portland and one hell of a recovery ahead, I finally decided it would be infeasible to have any substantial work experience.

This Summer 2026 collection is where I'll record the many mini-projects I inevitably find myself thinking up. I might also write about my favorite trips or hikes. This collection is meant to be more random than the rest of this website — less portfolio, more log. I might even write about the experience of building this website itself (I'm using Git + GitHub Actions to deploy it).
